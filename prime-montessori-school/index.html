How payments are confirmed

  1. The bursar uploads the official bank statement.
  2. Each credit is matched to a pupil's invoice by amount, date and narration.
  3. Unclear credits go to the review list rather than being guessed.
  4. Receipts and balances update only from confirmed credits.
