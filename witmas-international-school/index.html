About us

WITMAS International School is a private co-educational school, founded on Christian principles and open to children of all faiths. We offer education and care at pre-nursery, nursery and primary levels for ages 0 to 11, from England's Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS) curriculum through the Cambridge Core curriculum, with an abroad-study pathway to a British college in Oxford.
