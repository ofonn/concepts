About us

Founded on Christian principles, open to all faiths

WITMAS International School is a private co-educational school in GRA Phase 2, Port Harcourt. We offer English-quality education and care at pre-nursery, nursery and primary levels for children from birth to 11 years, with an abroad-study pathway to a British college in Oxford.

  • Co-educational, for children from all faiths
  • EYFS through the Cambridge Core curriculum
  • Pathway to a British college in Oxford, UK
