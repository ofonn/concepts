About DIS

A serene, purpose-built campus in Osong Ama

Large, well-ventilated classrooms. An administrative block, hall and sick bay, all joined by covered walkways so children move safely in any weather.

Our mission is an excellent learning environment where every child develops a positive self-image, social and cultural awareness, independence, self-sufficiency, integrity and perseverance.

Curriculum

  1. 01

    Nigerian Basic Education Curriculum

    The national curriculum from the Federal Ministry of Education is our foundation.

  2. 02

    US Common Core (CCSS)

    Related Common Core standards are incorporated across subjects.

  3. 03

    UK Unit Standards

    UK unit standards complete the Dove International Schools Limited Curriculum.

Learning philosophy

Outcomes-based Education and Training (OBET)

Every lesson is planned around specific outcomes in knowledge, skills and attitudes, using a performance descriptor written for each unit.

  • Pupils are helped to learn to their full potential
  • Success builds self-confidence as they progress
  • The classroom is set up so every learner can succeed
Teacher development. New teachers complete the core Educational Leadership courses before taking a class, with refreshers each session.
